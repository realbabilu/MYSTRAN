Element-specific formulation (preserved from earlier research manuscript)

7.8 MH6T v4 — triangular modified-field MacNeal family

Using P2 geometry, form direct bending from interpolated rotations and reconstruct in-layer/shear strains by a weighted projection over separately specified triangular sample points: \[ \bar B_\alpha(r,s)=\sum_{j=1}^{m}H_j(r,s)B^{n}_\alpha(r_j,s_j),\qquad K=\int_{\Omega_\triangle}(h\bar B_m^TC_m\bar B_m+\tfrac{h^3}{12}B_b^TC_mB_b+h\bar B_s^TC_s\bar B_s+h c_dB_d^TB_d)J_A\,dr\,ds. \] Adopted: modified/assumed-strain treatment of thin-shell locking. Modified: triangular tying and six-DOF drilling. Not reproduced: equivalence of this research interpolation to any single unmodified published MH6T element.

Applicable verification and limitations

See reference-anchored comparison, including 2-001 patch checks, 2-005 C/D convergence, 2-008 modal, 2-013 thermal stress, 2-014 thermal bending, 2-016 and 2-017 buckling. A solver pairing is not a reference accuracy proof. 2-009 foundation remains excluded.

  • Modal: frequency reference is 1 / period; compare ratio f_solver/f_ref and absolute frequency errors separately.
  • Thermal: local radial–tangential axes and resultants must match CSI definitions.
  • Buckling: preserve signed eigenvalues, prestress load basis and mode pairing; do not assume mode indices are interchangeable.
  • Solver caveats: report AUTOSPC/BAILOUT conditions where present.
